# What we collect

Guest Wi-Fi does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information.

To run the service and keep it secure, the network records:

- your device's hardware (MAC) address and network (IP) address;
- the times you connect, accept the terms and disconnect;
- the wireless access point and network your device used.

# Why

These records are used only to operate the guest network, to troubleshoot problems you report, and to investigate misuse or security incidents.

# How long

Records are kept for up to 30 days, unless they are needed longer for a security investigation. This sign-in page sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

# Monitoring

Guest Wi-Fi is a U.S. Government information system. Use is monitored and you have no expectation of privacy on it. See the notice on the sign-in page.
