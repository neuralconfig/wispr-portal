# Domain shortlist for the hosted portal product (undecided)

Round 1 (2026-11-08): all unregistered, all sold by Cloudflare Registrar. Prices are third-party tracker figures; confirm price and
premium status in the dashboard.
- **wifi-guest.com** $10.46: .com trust, explains itself in the sign-in sheet; hyphen, generic.
- **guestlobby.net** $11.86: most brandable; .io/.dev/.cloud/.us/.co also free; the .com is on Afternic. "Lobby" is crowded in
  visitor management.
- **acceptwifi.com** $10.46: no hyphen. wifiaccept.com is a live "WiFi Login" page.
- **wi-fi.dev** $8.20 / $12.20: premium unknown; ".dev" reads as development; it is the Wi-Fi Alliance mark itself.
- Others: wirelesslobby.com, lobbywireless.com, onetapguest.com ($10.46); joinguest.net, guestjoin.net, okwifi.net, tapwifi.net
  ($11.86); guestaccess.us, wifiguest.us ($6.50, US nexus, no WHOIS privacy); guestsignin.app, joinwifi.app, clickwifi.app
  ($8.20 / $14.20); wi-fi.page ($10.20); wifisignin.com (looks like phishing); lobbylogin.com (crowded); connectlobby.com
  (collides with Eptura's LobbyConnect).
- Not possible: the `.wifi` TLD doesn't exist. guestwifi, wifiguest, guestconnect, guestaccess and guestportal .com are taken
  (guestportal.com is a live hospitality SaaS).

Round 3 (2026-11-08): three parallel agents checked about 500 .com names through Verisign RDAP (404 means unregistered). The
top 14 were re-checked afterwards and were still unregistered. An unregistered .com is ~$10.46 at Cloudflare, but checkout must
confirm the price and that the name isn't premium. Collision checks were quick web searches, not trademark searches.
- **Combined top picks:**
  - **courtesywifi.com**: "courtesy Wi-Fi" is what hospitals and hotels already call it, so it's trusted in the captive sheet. 12 letters.
  - **frontdeskwifi.com** (also **wififrontdesk.com**): instantly understood by clinics and hotels. 13 letters.
  - **greetwifi.com**: short, spellable after hearing it once, brandable.
  - **arrivalwifi.com**: a warm "you've arrived" feel. Arrival was an EV maker, which isn't a Wi-Fi collision.
  - **wifiallset.com**: reassuring and healthcare-neutral, but no "guest".
- **Next tier:**
  - guestaccept.com (and acceptguest.com): describes the click-through.
  - joinasguest.com and wifiasguest.com.
  - hallwifi.com: 8 letters, but can be heard as "haul".
  - lobbyhello.com: pairs with lobbywifi.
  - okayguest.com: pairs with okaywifi.
  - hoponwifi.com: casual. A dormant 2005 "Hop-On" Wi-Fi phone and Whidbey Telecom's "HOP WiFi" exist.
  - guestswifi.com: the "s" gets lost aloud, and guestwifi.com is taken.
  - wififorguests.com.
- **Avoid:**
  - porchwifi and openporchwifi: Front Porch Inc. sells hotspot Wi-Fi services.
  - frontdoorwifi: Frontdoor Inc.
  - opendoorwifi: Opendoor.
  - hopinwifi: Hopin.
  - taponwifi: TapToWiFi and Tap2WiFi.
  - atriumwifi: CDVI Atrium access control.
  - enterwifi: reads as "enter your Wi-Fi password".
- **Also free:**
  - entrancewifi, wirelesswelcome, wifivisitor, foyerwireless, greetingwifi, wifigreet, wifiarrival, doorwaywifi, thresholdwifi,
    wifireception
  - joinguestwifi, guestwifiready, clicktowifi, oneclickwifi, agreeandgo, acceptandgo, allsetwifi, wifigreenlight, steptowifi
  - foyerhello, signalhello, hospeswifi, portaguest, wavelobby
- **No single-word coinage under ~8 letters is free:** guestly, guestify, joinly, porchly, wavely and airly are all taken.
- **Taken:**
  - welcomewifi, hellowifi, visitorwifi, conciergewifi, receptionwifi, checkinwifi
